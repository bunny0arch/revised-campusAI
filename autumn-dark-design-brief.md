# CampusFix Autumn-Dark Design Direction

CampusFix remains a public, first-level campus support workspace. Its primary action is still to describe an issue and begin a safe diagnosis; the visual change must therefore make the conversation, diagnostic stage, voice controls, and ticket status feel calm and legible rather than theatrical.

The palette moves from daylight oat and sage to a low-light **autumn study**: espresso and cocoa surfaces establish a quiet base; walnut boundaries provide structure; muted rust identifies active actions and unresolved escalation; warm oat carries readable copy; and an olive-sage reserve is retained only for successful, safe, or resolved states. Soft radial ember, maple, and tobacco gradients are limited to the page atmosphere and focal status controls. No blue, purple, neon glow, or high-transparency glass treatment is introduced.

Typography, composition, responsive structure, diagnostic stages, anonymous privacy language, voice selection, and ticket lifecycle controls remain unchanged. Contrast is deliberately strongest around the composer, active diagnosis state, ticket popover, and outcome control so a student can act confidently in a dim environment without losing the calm character of the interface.
