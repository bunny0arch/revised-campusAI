# CampusFix Public Interface — Design Audit

## Product frame

CampusFix is a public, first-level university IT support service. Its user is a student, faculty member, or staff member who needs practical help quickly, often while their access, network, printer, or software is impaired. The primary action is to describe a problem and proceed through one useful diagnostic step at a time.

## Visual direction: Service Console

The interface uses the visual language of a **quiet campus service console**, rather than a SaaS dashboard or an AI marketing page. A near-black navy field, fine technical grid, restrained cyan for actionable and live states, and a singular pale green safety signal make the product feel calm, accountable, and operational. The surface is deliberately opaque and bordered; it is not a floating glass card. Rounded corners remain limited to controls, message ownership, and the single support instrument.

## Hierarchy and interaction

The title communicates the promise, but the diagnostic console is the product’s functional center. The console begins with a clear safety and scope statement, exposes familiar issue starters for reduced cognitive load, and keeps the text composer permanently visible. The side rail visualizes the real diagnosis process—understand, verify, guide, confirm, escalate—rather than presenting abstract features. Motion is limited to new messages, streaming feedback, and status changes; it is functional feedback, not decoration.

## Type, color, spacing, and responsiveness

Space Grotesk gives the product a compact technical voice, while DM Sans keeps instructions readable. The color system reserves cyan for progress, inputs, and active controls; green is reserved for safety and successful resolution. Spacing opens the page for readability but tightens around the actual support task. On narrow screens, the console preserves the composer and outcome controls, removes redundant process exposition, and makes the primary action available without sign-in.

## Audit outcome

The interface does not use a generic SaaS dashboard, a repeated feature-card grid, gradient text, or an Inter-based template. This refinement removes the remaining nonessential blue ambient gradients and backdrop glass effect while leaving streaming diagnosis, voice assistance, anonymous persistence, safe outcome checks, and conditional ticket escalation unchanged.
